ExecutionNo automatic trading

HOW IT WORKS

One dashboard. Ranked opportunities.

Trend quality

Measures broader direction, structure and long-term trend behaviour.

Pullback analysis

Looks for retracements into relevant dynamic support or resistance areas.

Momentum alignment

Evaluates RSI behaviour, Bollinger Band positioning and indicator slope relationships.

Setup scoring

Combines the signals into a ranked score for manual review.
